# Snowdown — Condensed Game Brief

## Player fantasy
The best snowball fight you can imagine: fast, playful, skillful, social, readable, full of near-misses, great throws, catches, slides, ambushes, and revenge moments.

## Core loop
Move and read the arena → find/pack snow → predict another player's movement → charge and throw a physical snowball → dodge/catch incoming throws → earn score for hits → continue without lethal downtime → finish the short round with the highest score.

## Experience target
A new player understands the basics within one minute. Mastery comes from projectile prediction, arcs, movement, map knowledge, catch timing, charge timing, spatial awareness, audio cues, and flanking.

## What must feel excellent first
1. First-person movement and camera.
2. Snowball packing and charge-to-throw interaction.
3. Projectile arc, travel time, impact and readable misses.
4. Dodging/near-miss readability.
5. Head/body hit feedback and score clarity.
6. Low-friction retry/playtest loop.

## Prototype success question
Is moving, predicting, dodging, packing, throwing, and landing snowballs on other human players genuinely fun?
