# Snowdown — Art Direction & World

## Style target
**PROVISIONAL:** stylized 3D rather than photorealism. Exaggerated winter clothing, clean silhouettes, readable heads/hands, soft snow, expressive impact FX, and inviting environments.

## Character language
Characters should feel like people dressed for a snow day, not soldiers. Favor:
- puffy coats;
- scarves;
- beanies;
- mittens;
- winter boots;
- expressive body language;
- visible snow accumulation after repeated hits as cosmetic feedback.

Avoid tactical vests, firearms silhouettes, military helmets, blood, wounds, or combat uniforms as the default aesthetic.

## Competitive silhouette
Head and torso must remain readable at gameplay distances. Cosmetics may visually vary but must not alter hitboxes or create misleading silhouettes beyond an acceptable tolerance.

## Snowball FX
Projectile should remain visible against snow and sky through shading, shadow, subtle trail/powder, or outline treatment. Trails should not become laser-like.

## Impact FX
World hit: powder puff + decal/splat.  
Body hit: readable splat burst.  
Head hit: larger comedic burst, optional hat wobble/spin animation without removing competitive control for long.

## Environment
Cozy mountain/village/playground tone. Warm windows and wood contrast with cold outdoor snow. Indoor spaces are navigational/cover elements, not tactical bunkers.

## Snow deformation
**PLANNED, not prototype-critical:** footprints, slide tracks, disturbed snow, and depleted piles can improve world storytelling. Implement in tiers and never let visual fidelity block core gameplay performance.

## Violence rating goal
Presentation should remain clearly non-lethal and playful. Exact platform rating target is **TBD** and must be reviewed before commercial release.
