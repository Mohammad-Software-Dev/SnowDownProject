# Snowdown — Glossary

**Snowdown** — The game/project name; also acceptable as the name of the default FFA mode.

**Snowball** — A throwable gameplay projectile. Use instead of “bullet” or “ammo” in player-facing language.

**Packed snowball** — A snowball currently available in player inventory.

**Packing** — The action of creating a snowball from a valid snow source.

**Snow source** — World surface/volume from which a player is allowed to pack snowballs.

**Throw charge** — 0–1 normalized strength accumulated while preparing a throw.

**Quick toss** — Low-charge, fast-release throw.

**Power throw** — High-charge throw near maximum configured launch speed. Not a damage attack.

**Body hit** — Valid projectile impact on non-head competitive player hit region.

**Head hit / head splat** — Valid projectile impact on the head scoring region. Prefer “head hit/head splat” over shooter terminology in player-facing copy.

**Hit reaction** — Brief audiovisual/animation consequence after a hit; not a death or damage state.

**Catch** — Timed defensive action that intercepts an eligible incoming snowball.

**Streak** — Consecutive successful scoring hits before the player is hit.

**Round** — Timed scoring period inside a session.

**Session/instance** — A capped server world hosting a group of players and one or more rounds.

**Party** — Group of friends treated as one matchmaking unit.

**Private Snow World** — Invite/friends/custom session for free play and custom rounds.

**Sidegrade** — Unlockable play-style variation with both advantage and disadvantage; not a net permanent power increase.

**Server authoritative** — Server owns final truth for hit, score, inventory, and match state.

**PROVISIONAL** — Current default that should be implemented but remain tunable.

**LOCKED** — Product decision that should not be changed without explicit design revision.

**TBD** — Intentionally unresolved; do not silently convert into permanent design.
