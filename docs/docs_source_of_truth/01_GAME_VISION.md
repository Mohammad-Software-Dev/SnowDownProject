# Snowdown — Game Vision

## Elevator pitch
Snowdown is a competitive multiplayer 3D snowball-fighting game built around projectile prediction, dodging, spatial awareness, movement, and playful physics. It has the immediacy and readability of a first-person action game without guns, killing, gore, or elimination-centered combat.

## Product identity
**LOCKED:** The core fantasy is “the best snowball fight you can imagine”: fast, chaotic, skillful, funny, social, readable, and full of near-misses, perfect throws, catches, slides, ambushes, and revenge moments.

Snowdown should be understandable within one minute: move, find snow, pack snowballs, throw them at other players, avoid incoming throws, score more for harder hits, finish the round with the highest score.

## Design pillars
1. **Easy to learn, hard to master.** Basic throwing is obvious. Mastery comes from prediction, arcs, charge timing, map knowledge, movement, catch timing, and spatial awareness.
2. **Competitive without lethality.** Hits matter, but players are not killed, downed, or forced into long respawn loops.
3. **Movement and awareness matter.** A player who only aims forward should be vulnerable to flanks, audio cues, rooftops, and incoming projectiles.
4. **Physical projectiles, not bullets.** Snowballs have visible flight, arc, travel time, impact, and readable miss information.
5. **Fair competition.** Persistent progression does not increase permanent combat power.
6. **Social by default.** Parties, join-friend, private rooms, continuity, playful emotes, and low downtime matter.
7. **Joyful readability.** Effects should communicate game state clearly without military or violent visual language.

## Target experience
A 5–8 minute match should generate repeated micro-stories: “I barely dodged that,” “I caught it,” “I hit the head from across the square,” “I slid behind cover,” “I got them back,” “I heard someone behind me.”

## Audience
**PROVISIONAL:** Broad teen-and-up audience with family-friendly presentation. Competitive players should find mechanical depth, while casual groups should be able to laugh and participate without mastering an FPS meta.

## Non-goals
**LOCKED:** Snowdown is not:
- a military shooter with snowball skins;
- a damage/health optimization game;
- pay-to-win or grind-to-win;
- a battle royale in the initial product;
- a survival crafting game;
- a hyper-realistic snow simulator;
- a game where permanent unlocks increase base throw speed, range, capacity, accuracy, or movement speed.

## Emotional tone
Playful, energetic, welcoming, expressive, slightly exaggerated, wintry, cozy between matches, and chaotic during matches.

## Core visual/feedback vocabulary
Prefer: splat, puff, tumble, stumble, snow burst, scarf flutter, hat spin, whoosh, crunch, plop, thump.  
Avoid: kill, death, damage, blood, weapon, bullet, execution, fatality.
