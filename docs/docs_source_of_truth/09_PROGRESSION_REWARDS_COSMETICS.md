# Snowdown — Progression, Rewards & Cosmetics

## Fairness rule
**LOCKED:** Persistent progression is horizontal. It must not permanently increase base throw range, projectile speed, packing/fire rate, inventory capacity, movement speed, hitbox advantage, accuracy, or catch window.

## What improves over time
The player improves through mastery of arcs, timing, movement prediction, positioning, audio, catch timing, resource use, and map knowledge.

## Account progression
**PROVISIONAL:** Players earn account XP from completed matches, participation, objectives, and challenges. Level is primarily a record of engagement and an unlock gate for cosmetics/challenges, not power.

## Rewards
Preferred permanent rewards:
- jackets/coats;
- scarves;
- beanies/hats;
- mittens;
- boots;
- player banners;
- titles and badges;
- emotes;
- victory poses;
- snowball trails;
- impact/splat visual styles;
- catch and packing animations;
- Snow Lodge/private-world decorations later.

## Sidegrades
Alternative throw styles may exist only if they are balanced trade-offs.

Examples:
- Classic: balanced;
- Quick Toss: faster release, shorter maximum range;
- Pitcher: longer range, slower wind-up;
- Lobber: higher arc, slower travel.

**LOCKED:** No sidegrade can be intentionally superior in general combat. Sidegrades should be disabled from competitive modes if balance cannot be demonstrated.

## Challenges
Challenges should teach or celebrate mechanics, e.g. long-range head hit, successful catches, airborne hits, streak milestones, objective play, varied map routes.

## Economy
**TBD:** currency names, store structure, premium model, battle pass, direct purchase model. Monetization must never sell competitive stat advantages.

## Cosmetic readability constraints
- hats do not change head hitbox;
- bulky clothing does not change body hitbox;
- trails cannot obscure projectile visibility;
- impact effects cannot hide other players for a competitive duration;
- cosmetics must preserve team/color readability when team modes use color coding.
