# Snowdown — Game Modes

## Mode 1: Snowdown Free-for-All
**Status: LOCKED for first playable/public core.**

- Every player is an opponent for scoring purposes.
- Timed round, default 5 minutes.
- Highest score wins.
- No elimination.
- Standard body/head scoring applies.

## Mode 2: Team Snowfight
**Status: PLANNED.**

Two teams compete for combined score. Party cohesion is preferred. Friendly fire should be **non-scoring and non-disruptive by default** so friends cannot grief teammates with hit reactions. Optional friendly snow splats can remain cosmetic in private matches.

## Mode 3: Snowball Control
**Status: PLANNED.**

Teams contest one or more capture areas. Standing uncontested accumulates objective score. Snowball hits are primarily a pressure/displacement tool and may also grant a smaller personal/team bonus. Exact objective timings are TBD.

## Mode 4: Crown
**Status: PLANNED.**

One player holds a highly visible crown/hat marker. Hitting the crowned player grants bonus score and transfers the crown to the attacker. Crown carrier gains no raw combat buff by default. Transfer lockout should prevent instant ping-pong abuse; value TBD.

## Mode 5: Snowman
**Status: CONCEPT.**

Teams defend a large snowman objective and attack designated target zones on the opponent snowman. Player hits create space; objective hits generate victory progress. Avoid framing the snowman as “health”; use playful states such as buttons popping off, hat wobbling, or snow layers being knocked away.

## Private Free Play
**Status: PLANNED.**

No required score/win state. Friends can practice movement, throws, catches, emotes, and map exploration. Host may start formal rounds without forcing everyone to leave the world.

## Mode design requirements
Every mode must specify:
- player cap;
- team structure;
- join-in-progress policy;
- score/win condition;
- tie handling;
- spawn rules;
- friendly hit policy;
- whether standard hit score is enabled;
- world reset behavior.

No new mode should require lethal terminology or a health/death system unless the core product direction is formally changed.
